# Spec: low stock report

## Goal
Make the low stock list inclusive and ordered.

## Non-goals
- Anything not listed in the acceptance criteria.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1. lowStock includes items whose qty equals the threshold.
- AC-2. The names are returned in alphabetical order.
- AC-3. src/inventory.js keeps its CRLF line endings.

## Constraints
- Node built-ins only. CommonJS modules.
