# Spec: days in month

## Goal
daysInMonth must be right for every year.

## Non-goals
- Anything not listed in the acceptance criteria.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1. daysInMonth(1900, 2) is 28.
- AC-2. Every other year and month keeps returning the correct number of days.

## Constraints
- Node built-ins only. CommonJS modules.

- None.
